# Kiroween 2026 submission rules (must be satisfied by the final repo)

- Deadline: **2026-12-04 14:00 PT**. Only work created from 2026-10-30 is eligible.
- Public **GitHub** repo with all source, assets and build/run instructions.
- An **open source LICENSE file** at the root (shown in GitHub's About section).
- The **`/.kiro` directory must stay in the repo root** (specs, hooks, steering).
  Never add `.kiro` or its subfolders to `.gitignore`.
- A **working app URL** judges can open (the browser converter + player page).
- Demo video < 3 minutes on YouTube, showing the project working end-to-end on the target
  device. No third-party trademarks or copyrighted music.
- A write-up of **how Kiro was used**: specs, hooks, steering, MCP, property-based testing,
  custom agents, Kiro powers, IDE vs CLI. Keep notes in `docs/KIRO_USAGE.md` as we go
  (date, what we asked, what Kiro did, what we changed and why).
- Bonus prizes to remember: blog post (mention it is written for Kiroween 2026),
  social post starting with `#KiroweenHackathon26`, Best Startup Project (founder of Tiny Core Inc.).

Judging criteria to optimise for: Potential Impact, Technological Implementation (depth of
Kiro use), Design (complete product, not a proof of concept), Innovation, Presentation.
